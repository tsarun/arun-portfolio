Build case studies

How I frame the product, not just the technology.

01

FeatureIQ

Agentic competitor intelligence · In progress

Problem
Product teams lose time manually following competitor releases and market signals scattered across different sources.
Product decisions
Scoped the first product around continuous signal tracking rather than a broad research suite, with a Python service layer and a Next.js interface designed for focused review.
AI approach
Claude API and agentic workflows are used to track, process, and organize competitor product updates into useful market intelligence.
Current outcome
The architecture and product concept are actively being built. No usage, accuracy, or performance metrics are claimed yet.

02

WealthTracker

AI-enabled personal finance · Live build

Problem
Indian retail investors often move between separate sources for market tracking, IPO updates, and portfolio interpretation.
Product decisions
Focused the experience on India-specific NSE/BSE data, combined tracking and IPO alerts in one dashboard, and used AES-encrypted storage as a deliberate trust decision.
AI approach
AI-generated insights add a narrative interpretation layer to investment analytics, while Chart.js keeps the underlying market information visually inspectable.
Current outcome
A working independent build demonstrates end-to-end product ownership. User adoption and performance metrics have not been published.

03

Portfolio Compiler

AI portfolio generator · MVP in progress

Problem
Turning a resume and supporting documents into a coherent professional portfolio is manual, repetitive, and difficult to structure well.
Product decisions
Started with common source formats—PDF and Word—and separated document extraction from narrative generation so source material remains the foundation.
AI approach
pdf.js and Mammoth.js extract source content; AI-assisted generation then organizes that material into a structured professional narrative.
Current outcome
The SaaS MVP is in progress. The current evidence establishes the workflow and implementation direction, not adoption or commercial impact.
